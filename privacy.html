Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026  ·  Ridge Sound, a sole proprietorship of Gil Abrams  ·  Los Angeles, California, USA

This policy explains what personal data Ridge Sound collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect very little, we do not track you, and the plugins themselves collect nothing at all.

The plugin never connects to the internet. License verification happens entirely on your own computer using cryptography, not by checking in with a server. Our software makes no network connections, sends no telemetry, and reports nothing about you, your machine, or what you make with it.

1. Who is responsible

Ridge Sound, a sole proprietorship of Gil Abrams, Los Angeles, California, United States, is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: support@ridgesound.com.

Paddle.com Market Limited is our merchant of record and is a separate controller for the payment data it collects. Their privacy notice is at paddle.com/legal/privacy.

2. What we collect, and when

When you buy something

Paddle collects your name, email address, billing country, and payment details in order to take payment and work out the tax. We never receive your card details. What we receive from Paddle is limited to what we need to issue your license:

  • your name, or your email address if you did not give a name;
  • your transaction reference and invoice number;
  • which product you bought.

When we issue your license

We store a license record so you can re-download your license file later. It contains your transaction reference, the name that appears on the license, a one-way hash of your email address (a scrambled form we can match against but cannot read back), and the signed license file itself. This is held in Cloudflare Workers KV storage in encrypted form.

We keep license records indefinitely, because a customer who bought two years ago still needs to be able to get their license file back. You can ask us to delete yours — see section 6.

When you visit this website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no fingerprinting, and no third-party tracker. We do not know who you are or that you were here.

The site is served by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, who process standard server request logs (including IP addresses) for security and delivery. That happens at the infrastructure level and we do not build profiles from it.

If you open a checkout, Paddle's checkout software loads at that point and may set cookies necessary for the payment to work. That is governed by Paddle's privacy notice.

When you email us

We keep support correspondence so we have context if you write again. Our email is hosted by Google Workspace.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data. Not to anyone, for any price.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you explicitly ask to be told about new releases.
  • We do not track you across websites, and we do not run advertising.
  • We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.
  • Our software does not scan your projects, your files, or your audio.

4. Legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract for issuing and re-issuing your license; legal obligation for records Paddle must keep for tax; and legitimate interests for answering support email and keeping our infrastructure secure.

5. Who else processes your data

  • PaddleMerchant of record — payment, invoicing, tax
  • CloudflareLicense portal hosting and license record storage
  • GitHub PagesWebsite hosting
  • Google WorkspaceOur email

These providers act on our behalf or as independent controllers for their own part of the process. We do not add anyone else without updating this page.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent. Residents of California have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know and the right to delete; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there.

To exercise any of these, email support@ridgesound.com. We will respond within 30 days. We will not ask you for anything beyond what we need to confirm the request is really yours.

One practical note: if you ask us to delete your license record, you lose the ability to re-download your license file from the portal. Keep a backup of the file first. The plugin will keep working — it verifies offline and does not need us.

7. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers operate globally. Where data moves out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our providers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Security

License records are stored with a major infrastructure provider and served only over HTTPS. The private key used to sign licenses is held offline and never leaves our control. Email addresses in license records are stored as one-way hashes rather than in readable form.

9. Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, requests, or complaints: support@ridgesound.com. If you are in the EEA or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.
