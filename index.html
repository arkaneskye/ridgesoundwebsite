A plugin is a piece of software that runs inside a music production application, the sort of thing people mean when they say DAW. Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Pro Tools. It sits in the signal path and changes the sound on its way through. Ours install as VST3 on Windows and as Audio Unit on macOS. Everything here runs unlocked in demo mode for as long as you like, so you can hear it on your own material first.
